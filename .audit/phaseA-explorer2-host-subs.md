- **Exact parameters & pipeline** ([SubtitleSyncAndManifest.kt:33-40, 176-450](/home/mahmood/.t3/worktrees/cloudstream/urdu-bolchal-audio-sync-17/app/src/main/java/com/lagradost/cloudstream3/utils/downloader/SubtitleSyncAndManifest.kt:33)):

  1. **Sub-1.5s lip-sync lag (`150ms..1,499ms`)**: Blocked twice — `abs(bestOffsetMs) in 1_500L..45_000L` (`:274`) hard-forbids shifts `< 1,500ms`, AND `OVERLAP_TOLERANCE_MS = 1_500L` (`:33`, `:439`) counts a `1,400ms`-lagged track as an exact hit at `0ms`, inflating `zeroScore` near `1.0` so `bestScore >= zeroScore + 0.18` (`:277`) is mathematically impossible.

Shifts `> 90s` miss `MAX_SEARCH_OFFSET_MS` completely.

Zero affine `(scale, offset)` solver exists.
  4. **Commercial-break / multi-act piecewise jumps & missing audio VAD**: `shiftVttTimestamps` (`:141-166`) applies one scalar `offsetMs` to the whole file; multi-act trims (`+0s` Act 1, `+9s` Act 2, `+19s` Act 3) either fail `hasStableHalfOffsets` (`:422`) or leave later acts desynced.
