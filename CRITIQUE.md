## Resolved Round 2 Council Defect Queue (`N1`–`N8`) & Round 3 Precision Items (`P1`–`P4`)

1. **`N1` & `P1` (Fixed): Exact Test, Resource & Contrast Citations + Full `:StreamPlay:testDebugUnitTest` Execution:**
   - Synchronized all test counts (`11` backtick-named tests in `StreamPlaySettingsUiUxSotaTest.kt`, `613` total tests across all `40` test classes in `:StreamPlay:testDebugUnitTest`), exact resource counts (`14` active layouts in `StreamPlay/src/main/res/layout/`, `4` active drawables in `StreamPlay/src/main/res/drawable/`), and all 8 exact WCAG contrast ratios (`17.26:1`, `6.67:1`, `5.44:1`, `5.56:1`, `5.17:1`, `9.48:1`, `8.29:1`, and `13.87:1` for `borderFocus` vs `surfaceCard`).
2. **`N2` & `P3` (Fixed): XML-Driven Screenshot Renderer, Single TV Focus Ring, Mirrored RTL Segment Track & Preserved Round History:**
   - Updated `scripts/render_settings_screenshots.py` to parse `StreamPlay/src/main/res/layout/*.xml` and `save_icon.xml` directly via `xml.etree.ElementTree`, render a single `2dp` `#E2E8F0` focus ring on `Concurrency` in `main-settings-tv-focus-500w.png`, mirror `English` (right/start) and `Urdu اردو` (left/end) in `main-settings-rtl-375w.png`, and write only to the active round directory (`screenshots/round-3/`) so historical round directories (`screenshots/round-0/`, `screenshots/round-1/`, `screenshots/round-2/`) are preserved for visual diffing.
3. **`N3` (Fixed): Complete Concurrency Tier Unification (`tv_mode` & `concurrencyLabel`):**
   - Removed `"Balanced (Recommended)"` from `ConcurrencyBottomSheet.kt` and `concurrency_bottom_sheet.xml` (`tv_mode` uses `StreamPlayConcurrency.concurrencyTier(currentValue)`), and updated `StreamPlayConcurrency.concurrencyLabel(value)` to delegate directly to `concurrencyTier(value)` (`Stable`, `Balanced`, `Fast`, `Max`).
4. **`N4` & `P2` (Fixed): Eliminated Engineering Jargon in Settings Copy & Scoped Player Provenance:**
   - Replaced `"· Neural fallback"` with `"· Streams and downloads"` in `MainSettingsFragment.kt` and `LanguageSelectFragment.kt`, replaced `"JSON tiers"` in `wyzie_settings_fragment.xml` with plain user copy, and removed `"SOTA"` / `"Neural AI"` from `UrduSubtitleEngine.sotaSourcesSummary()`. (Internal Kotlin symbol names and the player-side subtitle track provenance label `Urdu [Neural AI]` in the video player's track picker are intentionally retained to distinguish machine-translated fallback tracks from human-authored tracks.)
5. **`N5` (Fixed): Removed 9 Unreferenced Drawables, Unused Tokens & Duplicated Dialog Boilerplate:**
   - Deleted the 9 unreferenced XML drawables, removed unused `STATUS_SUCCESS`/`STATUS_WARNING` and no-op `cornerRadius` in `StreamPlayDesignTokens.kt`, and centralized `configureDialogWindow`, `applyWindowInsetsPadding`, `applyDirectionalFocus`, and `restartHostApp`.
6. **`N6` (Fixed): Preset Name Encoding in `ProvidersFragment.kt`:**
   - Added `ProvidersFragment.encodeProfilesMap` and `decodeProfilesMap` using UTF-8 URL-encoding on preset keys so preset names containing `:`, `|`, or `,` round-trip cleanly without corrupting `provider_profiles`.
7. **`N7` (Fixed): Preserved `MainSettingsFragment` Scroll State & Bounded `stremio_dialog_list_links.xml` Height:**
   - Stopped calling `dismiss()` in `MainSettingsFragment.showSubFragment` so the parent dialog stays mounted underneath and preserves its scroll position when sub-dialogs close; gave `rvLinks` in `stremio_dialog_list_links.xml` `android:layout_height="0dp" android:layout_weight="1"` and capped dialog height at `85%` of screen height.
8. **`N8` & `P4` (Fixed / Declared): Live Regions, Initial TV Focus, RTL Focus Inversion, `DiffUtil` & Runtime Verification Scope:**
   - Added `android:accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` on dynamic status/count `TextView`s across layouts, added `<requestFocus />` on `subtitle_mode_pill_default` in `fragment_main_settings.xml`, added RTL-aware horizontal focus linking via `applyDirectionalFocus`, and replaced `notifyDataSetChanged()` with `DiffUtil.calculateDiff` in `LanguageSelectFragment.LanguageAdapter`.
